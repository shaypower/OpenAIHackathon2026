I built and ran a working map prototype for the 45-minute healthcare-access scenario.
Illustrative simulation results: the baseline reaches 48 of 110 modeled residents within 45 minutes (43.6%). The assumed feeder links bring all 110 within the target. The baseline gaps are Borrisoleigh (51 min), Silvermines (49 min) and Upperchurch (58 min). Under the hypothetical flood closures, those three links have no path; adding the candidate links restores access for all 110 in this model.
The map and API ran successfully and returned 29 GeoJSON features. Start the app with uvicorn backend.main:app --reload, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000/. The CLI command is python -m backend.simulate_demo --mode intervention --target 45.
